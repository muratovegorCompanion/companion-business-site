## Confirm before adding public wording

NBU Regulation No. 173 requires the following information before an insurance contract is concluded. Some items only apply if the broker actually performs the relevant activity. These facts must be confirmed by management before publishing definitive statements.

1. **Classes of insurance actually distributed by the broker.**
   Need the exact statutory class list, not marketing categories such as DMS, logistics, property and liability.

2. **Separate subdivisions where a consumer may conclude an insurance contract.**
   Confirm whether the company has any legally registered separate subdivisions. If none, publish an explicit statement that there are none.

3. **Receipt of insurance premiums by the broker.**
   Confirm whether Companion is authorised under insurer mandate agreements to receive consumer premiums into a special-use current account. If not, state that premiums are not accepted by the broker and are paid directly according to insurer/contract instructions.

4. **Insurance payouts through the broker.**
   Confirm whether any products allow insurance payouts to pass through a broker special-use current account. If not, state that the broker does not make insurance payouts and payouts are made by the insurer in accordance with the insurance contract.

5. **Assistance services supplied by the broker itself.**
   Confirm whether Companion legally provides assistance services itself, or only coordinates insurer/assistance-company services. The public description must match the contractual model.

6. **Claims-settlement support.**
   The website says Companion supports clients after policy placement. Confirm the actual process: initial notice, document assistance, communication with insurer/assistance, escalation, and limits of the broker's authority. Regulation No. 173 requires the broker to disclose the procedure if it accompanies a consumer during claims settlement.

7. **Other authorised state-body contacts.**
   Confirm which additional bodies and contact routes the company's legal/compliance policy uses for consumer complaints, then add them beside NBU contact information.

8. **Product-by-product disclosure.**
   For each standard product actually distributed, add the insurer's official product-information link. If information differs by insurer, disclose items 1-5 separately for each insurer as required.

## Maintenance

- Website, mobile version and mobile application disclosures should stay aligned.
- Do not publish operational statements about premiums, payouts, assistance or subdivisions until the underlying contractual/legal fact is confirmed.
- Update required public disclosures promptly when company or product information changes.
